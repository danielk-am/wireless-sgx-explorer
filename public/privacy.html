Wireless@SGX Explorer

Privacy and terms

Wireless@SGX Explorer is an independent guide by danielk.am. Contact: hey@danielk.am.

Your searches and location

Catalogue searches run against our listed venues. Nearby lookups send your chosen coordinates to this service to calculate distances. The application does not store search text or coordinates, and does not send them to advertisers. Hosting and network providers process connection information such as IP addresses to serve and protect the site.

“Use my location” requires your browser’s permission. It is optional. You can instead choose a place, enter coordinates or select a map point.

Google place search and maps

When enabled, Google place search loads after you choose “Search any Singapore place”; Google Maps loads after you choose “Show map”. Google receives connection information, typed searches and the map area you view. Selecting a suggestion retrieves its address and coordinates from Google, then sends the coordinates to our service for nearby hotspot lookup. These details are not added to our downloadable hotspot catalogue.

Google features are subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

When Google is not configured, the optional map uses OpenStreetMap tiles. Its provider receives your IP address and viewed map area. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Using this guide

Hotspot listings come from the attributed data.gov.sg catalogue. Google resolves starting locations; it does not verify hotspot operation. Source dates are shown in the explorer. Listing does not guarantee current Wi-Fi, access, opening hours or signal coverage. Distances are approximate straight-line distances, not walking routes. Check with the venue before relying on connectivity.

Advertising is labelled and does not affect ranking. This guide is not affiliated with IMDA or the network operators.
