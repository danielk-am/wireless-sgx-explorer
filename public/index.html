KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

What to know
before you go.

Wi-Fi Explorer helps you find venues listed in IMDA’s historical Wireless HotSpots dataset on data.gov.sg. Each pin groups entries at one venue. Open a listing to check its floor or area before heading over.

Use the pins to shortlist nearby venues. Before visiting, confirm Wi-Fi service, visitor access and opening hours with the venue. For a working stop, ask about seats and power sockets too. These details need a venue check because the historical records do not establish them. Signal coverage also needs checking on arrival; the source gives venue locations rather than a measured Wi-Fi radius.

A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Finding your next
Wi-Fi stop

How do I find Wireless@SGX hotspots near a place?

Enter a Singapore place, address or postal code and select a Google suggestion. The Explorer uses that starting point to sort listed venues by distance. You can also choose “Use my location” or pick a point on the map.

How can I check Wi-Fi before visiting?

Start with the listed floor or area, then ask the venue whether Wi-Fi is available to visitors today. These listings come from a historical catalogue, so confirming service and access is especially useful when you’re making a trip for Wi-Fi.

Is the distance a walking route or Wi-Fi range?

The distance is an approximate straight line from your starting point to the venue. Open the walking directions link to plan your route. For Wi-Fi, check the listed floor or area and test the signal when you arrive; the displayed distance measures proximity to the venue, not signal reach.

Can I use these places for remote work?

Use the listings to shortlist a working stop. If you need to take a call or work for a few hours, ask the venue about visitor access, seating, sockets and a suitable space. The catalogue lists hotspot locations; those working facilities and current connection quality need a separate check.
